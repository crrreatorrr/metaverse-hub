## Final benchmark method and evidence addendum

PR161 satisfies the original tooling criterion: “Harness is reproducible and does
not claim capacity until run on the intended hardware.” Its normal merge does not
mean every workload passed. All five backend harness jobs passed at exact f12;
the primary-client workflow failed and remains required evidence for the later
operating-limit decision. `ci_advisory` reports select no production limits,
capacity, hosting purchase or rollout authority.

The primary population is one actual public DOM campaign plus twenty independent
protocol actors and one shared 20-Hub/10-free-PvP background. Backend retry,
cold/warm and sustained profiles use 20/40/60 actors with explicit source,
submitted, accepted and verified numerators. Planned abandonment contributes only
its 120 submitted frames; complete copies of its source also run elsewhere.
Legacy comparisons preserve their unchanged budgets, actual failures and
per-segment receipt meaning. The manual-only failed twenty-renderer stress is a
separate experiment, not a passing primary lane.

Exact-match authoritative RESULT may cancel only its own pending metric read.
Reports retain match identity proof, original request/end timestamps and explicit
censor denominators. This does not erase previously completed live deadline
failures or unknown-timing reads in the preserved earlier runs. Likewise, the
primary's clean browser close and healthy observations do not resolve its
stalled result RPCs. The full 9634 actual-Game/retained-service correctness proof
above remains separate from this failed under-load prefix.

PR21 requires intended-host execution, concrete hosting approval, economic checks
and the explicit provenance decision. PR22 remains gated on PR21; no public
activation or production deployment is authorized by these merges.
